3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly dependent on sequence deviations, as Trace 9 shows both correct and incorrect control-flow with policy violations.
  • Binding and activity policy violations are observed together in Trace 9, indicating that lifecycle and resource assignment errors often co-occur.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common among the detected issues in this dataset.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to explicitly prohibit 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce correct activity order, especially ensuring 'Close Ticket' is always the final step.
  • Implement strict lifecycle state checks to prevent Pivot activities like 'Request Support' from reactivating after reaching 'done'.
  • Configure resource management to block resource consumption if the associated activity is not in the 'activated' state, and ensure resources cannot be reused after reaching a terminal state.
  • Enforce binding rules so that only activities in the 'activated' state can consume resources, rejecting any binding attempts from activities in 'done' or other terminal states.
