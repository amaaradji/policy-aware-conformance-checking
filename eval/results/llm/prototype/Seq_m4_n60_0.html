1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains an extra activity 'Unexpected Activity' not in the model.
  • Trace 4: 'Add to Cart' occurs after 'Checkout', which is out of the expected order.
  • Trace 6: Missing 'Add to Cart' activity before 'Checkout'.
  • Trace 8: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.
  • Trace 10: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation—'Search Products' using 'Product Catalog Database' failed without being activated, violating the Retriable lifecycle.
  • Trace 2: Resource policy violation—'Login to Account' used 'User Authentication Service' without enforcing the required resource policy.
  • Trace 4: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 7: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 9: Binding policy violation—'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Search Products' failed without proper activation, violating the activity lifecycle policy.
  • Trace 7: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Add to Cart' used an incorrect resource, violating the binding policy.
  • Trace 9: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' used an incorrect resource, violating the binding policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of sequence deviations, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but compliance issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, affecting three separate traces.
  • Control-flow issues and policy violations may overlap but are not strictly correlated.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to enforce the correct order: 'Search Products' must always precede 'Add to Cart' and 'Checkout'.
  • Implement strict lifecycle state checks for Retriable activities to ensure they are activated before failure and only retried within allowed limits.
  • Enforce resource policy checks for 'User Authentication Service' to ensure exclusive access and proper state transitions before reuse.
  • Restrict activity-resource assignments so that only the correct, bound resource (e.g., 'Shopping Cart Service' for 'Add to Cart') can be consumed by each activity.
