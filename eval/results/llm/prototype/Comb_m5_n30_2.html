3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of control-flow deviations, as seen in Trace 8, which is sequence-perfect but has a lifecycle violation.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the BPMN model to explicitly disallow or handle 'Unexpected Activity' after 'Verify Income' and after 'Reject Loan' to prevent extra activities.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Enforce strict lifecycle transitions for retriable activities, ensuring 'failed' cannot transition directly to 'done' without an authorized retry.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Review and configure the 'Income Verification Service' to track and enforce correct usage patterns, preventing unauthorized state transitions during activity execution.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Implement validation to ensure only activities in the 'Activated' state can consume resources, blocking any resource assignment outside this state.
