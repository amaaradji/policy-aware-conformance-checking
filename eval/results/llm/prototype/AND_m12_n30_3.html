1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains an extra activity ('Unexpected Activity') not present in the BPMN model.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 5: Activity 'Notify Team' with resource 'System A' violated the Activity Policy by performing an unauthorized failed→activated transition.
  • Trace 6: Activity 'Assign Priority' with resource 'Database C' violated the Binding Policy due to a missing binding policy during resource assignment.
  • Trace 10: Activity 'Assign Priority' with resource 'Database C' violated the Binding Policy due to a missing binding policy during resource assignment.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 5: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Notify Team' violated the Activity Policy by attempting an unauthorized lifecycle transition.
  • Trace 6 and Trace 10: Both traces follow the correct activity order but failed compliance due to missing binding policies for 'Assign Priority' with 'Database C'.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of activity sequence deviations, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but compliance issues.
  • Binding Policy violations are the most common, appearing in two separate traces for the same activity-resource pair.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to explicitly disallow unmodeled activities such as 'Unexpected Activity' to prevent control-flow deviations.
  • Enforce strict lifecycle transitions for Pivot activities by blocking unauthorized state changes like failed→activated for 'Notify Team'.
  • Review resource usage rules for all NonShareable and Limited resources to ensure exclusive access and proper state transitions, even though no resource policy violations were reported in this batch.
  • Implement mandatory binding policy checks before allowing 'Assign Priority' to consume 'Database C', ensuring only Activated activities can bind resources.
