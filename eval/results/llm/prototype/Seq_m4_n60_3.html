  • Trace 4: 'Add to Cart' occurs after 'Checkout', which is out of the expected order.
  • Trace 6: Missing 'Add to Cart' activity before 'Checkout'.
  • Trace 8: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of the expected order.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 2: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Login to Account' with resource 'User Authentication Service' violated resource policy due to missing resource policy enforcement.
  • Trace 4: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', violating binding policy.
  • Trace 7: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', violating binding policy.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Search Products' failed to activate due to unauthorized access, violating the activity policy.
  • Trace 7: The activity order is correct, but 'Add to Cart' used an incorrect resource, violating the binding policy.
  • Trace 9: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' used an incorrect resource, violating the binding policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of sequence deviations, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but compliance issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, appearing in three separate traces.
  • Control-flow and policy violations are not strongly correlated; both can occur separately or together.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce the correct order of 'Search Products' before 'Add to Cart' and ensure 'Checkout' is always last.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for 'Search Products', ensuring only authorized activations can proceed.
  • Enforce resource policy checks for 'User Authentication Service', including proper state transitions and usage caps before granting access.
  • Restrict activities to consume only their bound resources by validating resource assignments at activation time for all activities.
