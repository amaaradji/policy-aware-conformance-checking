3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of sequence deviations, as seen in Trace 1, which followed the correct activity order but still violated multiple policies.
  • Activity Policy violations are the most common, particularly related to retry limits for 'Credit Check' with 'Credit Bureau API'.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to enforce mandatory execution of 'Risk Assessment' before 'Approve and Disburse' to prevent missing activities.
  • Implement strict retry counters in the 'Credit Check' activity logic to block activation after three failed attempts.
  • Configure the 'Credit Bureau API' resource to automatically lock or reject requests exceeding the 5-minute usage cap and prevent reuse after entering the 'Done' state.
  • Enforce binding rules so that only activities in the 'Activated' state can consume resources, and ensure resources are not transitioned to 'unlocked' or 'done' while the activity is already 'done'.
