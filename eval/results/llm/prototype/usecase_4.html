1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Missing 'Risk Assessment' activity compared to the model sequence.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity Policy violation in 'Credit Check' with resource 'Credit Bureau API'—the activity exceeded the allowed retry count (more than 3 retries).
  • Trace 1: Resource Policy violation in 'Credit Check' with resource 'Credit Bureau API'—the resource was held or consumed longer than the allowed maximum elapsed time (over 5 minutes).
  • Trace 1: Binding Policy violation in 'Credit Check' with resource 'Credit Bureau API'—the activity-resource association exceeded the allowed consumption time (over 5 minutes) and state transitions occurred while the activity was not in the correct state.
  • Trace 1: Binding Policy violation in 'Risk Assessment' with resource 'Risk Analyst'—resource state transitions (consumed→unlocked, unlocked→done) occurred while the activity was already in 'done' state.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1 has perfect control-flow conformance but is non-compliant due to exceeding retry limits for 'Credit Check', resource overuse, and improper activity-resource state transitions.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur even when the activity sequence perfectly matches the model, as seen in Trace 1.
  • Sequence deviations and policy violations are independent; a trace may have one without the other.
  • Binding Policy violations are the most common, affecting both 'Credit Check' and 'Risk Assessment' in Trace 1.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model change: Update the process model to allow for optional execution of 'Risk Assessment' if business rules permit skipping it.
  • Activity Policy fix: Enforce a strict retry limit for 'Credit Check' by implementing a check that blocks further activations after three failures.
  • Resource Policy fix: Configure 'Credit Bureau API' to automatically release or deny access if usage exceeds 5 minutes, and prevent reuse after reaching the 'Done' state.
  • Binding Policy fix: Ensure that resource state transitions (unlock, done) are only triggered when the associated activity is in the correct lifecycle state (e.g., not after activity is 'done').
