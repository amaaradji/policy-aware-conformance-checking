3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of control-flow deviations, as shown by Trace 9, which has perfect conformance but a binding policy violation.
  • Control-flow issues (such as missing or extra activities) do not necessarily lead to policy violations, and vice versa.
  • The most common violation type in this dataset is the binding policy violation, as only this type appears in the summary.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the BPMN model to explicitly allow or restrict 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce the mandatory presence of 'Assign Priority' where required.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Implement stricter lifecycle state checks to ensure all activities follow their defined activation and completion paths without unauthorized retries or compensations.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Review and enforce resource usage rules, ensuring resources are not reused after reaching a terminal 'Done' state and that usage caps are respected.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Enforce validation so that activities can only consume resources to which they are correctly bound at the time of activation, preventing substitution with unbound resources.
