2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 9: Although the activity sequence is correct, the 'Resolve Incident' activity incorrectly used 'HR System' instead of the assigned 'Person I', resulting in a binding policy violation.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of sequence deviations, as seen in Trace 9, which has perfect control-flow but a binding policy violation.
  • Binding policy violations are the only type observed in this dataset, indicating that incorrect activity-resource assignments are the most common issue.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the process model to explicitly allow or restrict 'Unexpected Activity' occurrences and enforce mandatory execution of 'Assign Priority' before 'Resolve Incident'.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Implement stricter lifecycle state checks to ensure all activities follow their required activation and completion paths without skipping or repeating states.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Enforce resource usage rules by configuring systems to prevent resource sharing or reuse beyond defined limits and ensure resources in 'Done' state are withdrawn.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Introduce automated validation to ensure that only the correct, pre-assigned resource is used by each activity at runtime, blocking unauthorized resource substitutions.
