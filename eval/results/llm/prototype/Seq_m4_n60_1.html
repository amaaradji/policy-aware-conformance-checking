1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains an extra 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 4: 'Add to Cart' occurs after 'Checkout', which is out of the modeled order.
  • Trace 6: Missing 'Add to Cart' activity before 'Checkout'.
  • Trace 8: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.
  • Trace 10: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - 'Search Products' with resource 'Product Catalog Database' failed without being activated, violating the retriable lifecycle.
  • Trace 2: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Login to Account' with resource 'User Authentication Service' lacked a required resource policy, violating non-shareable limited usage rules.
  • Trace 4: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', violating binding policy.
  • Trace 7: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', violating binding policy.
  • Trace 9: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service', violating binding policy.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Search Products' failed without proper activation, breaching the activity lifecycle policy.
  • Trace 7: The activity order is correct, but 'Add to Cart' incorrectly used 'Accounting System' instead of its bound resource, violating the binding policy.
  • Trace 9: The sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the correct resource, violating the binding policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur even when the activity sequence is correct, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but compliance issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, appearing in three separate traces.
  • Sequence deviations and policy violations are largely independent, as both can occur separately or together.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce the correct order: ensure 'Add to Cart' always precedes 'Checkout' and remove allowance for unexpected activities.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for retriable activities, ensuring 'Search Products' cannot fail without first being activated.
  • Enforce resource access controls for 'User Authentication Service' to ensure non-shareable and limited usage policies are always applied and monitored.
  • Strengthen binding enforcement so that activities can only consume their specifically bound resources, rejecting mismatched resource assignments at runtime.
