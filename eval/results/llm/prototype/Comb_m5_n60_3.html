1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: 'Approve Loan' occurs after 'Notify Applicant', which is out of order compared to the model.
  • Trace 6: Contains an 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 7: Contains an 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 9: Missing 'Approve Loan' or 'Reject Loan' activity before 'Notify Applicant'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation on 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—unauthorized lifecycle transition (activated→unknown).
  • Trace 2: Binding policy violations on 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource state changes occurred while activity was already done.
  • Trace 3: Resource policy violation on 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource policy was absent during usage.
  • Trace 6: Binding policy violation on 'Verify Income'—resource 'Approval Board' used instead of the bound 'Income Verification Service'.
  • Trace 7: Resource policy violations on 'Reject Loan' with 'Rejection Letter Template'—resource was overused and withdrawn without authorization.
  • Trace 8: Resource policy violation on 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—resource policy was absent during usage.
  • Trace 10: Activity policy violations on 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—unauthorized transitions to and from 'compensated' state.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Verify Income' made an unauthorized lifecycle transition, violating the activity policy.
  • Trace 3: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Approve Loan' used 'Loan Approval System' without an enforced resource policy.
  • Trace 8: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Verify Income' used 'Income Verification Service' without an enforced resource policy.
  • Trace 10: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Verify Income' performed unauthorized lifecycle transitions involving the 'compensated' state.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of sequence deviations, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but compliance issues.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces through absent policies or improper resource usage.
  • Binding and activity policy violations also occur but are less frequent than resource policy violations.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to explicitly restrict or handle unexpected activities and enforce correct ordering between 'Approve Loan', 'Reject Loan', and 'Notify Applicant'.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for 'Verify Income', ensuring only authorized transitions (e.g., no direct transitions to 'unknown' or 'compensated' states for Retriable activities).
  • Enforce resource policies for all resources, including 'Loan Approval System' and 'Income Verification Service', to prevent absent policies and unauthorized usage or state transitions.
  • Validate activity-resource bindings at runtime to ensure only the correct, bound resource is used by each activity and that resource state changes occur only when the activity is in the appropriate state.
