1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains an extra 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 4: 'Add to Cart' occurs after 'Checkout', which is out of order compared to the model.
  • Trace 6: Missing 'Add to Cart' activity before 'Checkout'.
  • Trace 8: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.
  • Trace 10: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation—'Search Products' with resource 'Product Catalog Database' failed authorization before activation.
  • Trace 2: Resource policy violation—'Login to Account' with 'User Authentication Service' lacked a required resource policy.
  • Trace 4: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 7: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 9: Binding policy violation—'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Search Products' failed an activity policy check due to unauthorized access before activation.
  • Trace 7: The sequence is correct, but 'Add to Cart' violated the binding policy by using 'Accounting System' instead of 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 9: The sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' violated the binding policy by using 'Accounting System' instead of 'User Authentication Service'.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations and sequence deviations are independent, as several traces have perfect conformance but still violate policies.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, occurring in Traces 4, 7, and 9.
  • Resource and activity policy violations are less frequent and appear in only one trace each.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce the correct order: ensure 'Search Products' always precedes 'Add to Cart' and 'Checkout', and remove allowance for unexpected activities.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle checks for 'Search Products' to prevent unauthorized transitions before activation.
  • Enforce resource policy presence and correct state transitions for 'User Authentication Service', ensuring it cannot be used without an active policy or after reaching a terminal state.
  • Restrict activity-resource assignments so that only the correct, bound resource (e.g., 'Shopping Cart Service' for 'Add to Cart') can be used during activity execution.
