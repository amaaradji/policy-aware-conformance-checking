1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Missing 'Close Ticket' activity compared to the model.
  • Trace 3: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' and omits 'Fix Hardware', deviating from the model.
  • Trace 4: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' and omits 'Fix Hardware', deviating from the model.
  • Trace 6: 'Replace Hardware' occurs before 'Request Support', which is out of order.
  • Trace 9: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' after 'Update Software', not present in the model.
  • Trace 10: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' after 'Close Ticket', not present in the model.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 9: [BINDING POLICY] - Activity 'Request Support' with resource 'Resource H': Resource was consumed while the activity was already done.
  • Trace 9: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - Activity 'Request Support' with resource 'Resource H': Activity transitioned from 'done' back to 'activated', which is not allowed for Pivot activities.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all perfectly conforming traces are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly tied to sequence deviations, as Trace 9 shows both a sequence deviation and policy violations, while other traces with sequence issues do not have policy violations.
  • Among the violation types, activity and binding policy violations are observed, with no resource policy violations present in this dataset.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to explicitly handle or restrict 'Unexpected Activity' occurrences and enforce correct activity order, especially before 'Close Ticket'.
  • Enforce strict lifecycle transitions for Pivot activities to prevent reactivation after reaching the 'done' state.
  • Review resource state management to ensure resources are not reused or consumed after their terminal state, even though no resource policy violations were detected in this report.
  • Implement validation to ensure resources are only consumed when the associated activity is in the 'Activated' state, preventing binding policy violations.
