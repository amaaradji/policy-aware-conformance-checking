1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' before 'Request Support', which is not in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 3: Missing 'Fix Hardware', 'Replace Hardware', or 'Update Software' between 'Install Software' and 'Close Ticket'.
  • Trace 4: Missing 'Request Support' before 'Install Software', and lacks hardware-related activities.
  • Trace 5: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' after 'Fix Hardware', which is not in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 8: Missing 'Close Ticket' after 'Replace Hardware'.
  • Trace 9: Starts with 'Fix Hardware' before 'Request Support', which is out of order.
  • Trace 10: Missing 'Close Ticket' after 'Replace Hardware'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Resource policy violation—activity 'Close Ticket' used resource 'Worker E' (NonShareableUnlimited) without enforcing exclusive access or proper resource state.
  • Trace 3: Resource policy violation—activity 'Request Support' used resource 'Resource H' (ShareableLimitedBR) without enforcing usage cap or correct sharing rules.
  • Trace 9: Resource policy violation—activity 'Close Ticket' used resource 'Worker E' (NonShareableUnlimited) without enforcing exclusive access or proper resource state.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all perfectly conforming traces are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations, as some traces with control-flow issues do not have policy violations and vice versa.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces regardless of their activity sequence correctness.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the BPMN model to explicitly disallow 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce mandatory 'Close Ticket' after hardware/software actions.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Implement stricter lifecycle checks to ensure activities follow their required state transitions without skipping or repeating states.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Configure 'Worker E' and 'Resource H' to enforce exclusive access, respect usage caps, and prevent reuse after reaching terminal states.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Enforce that only activities in the 'Activated' state can consume resources, rejecting assignments from other states.
