1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains an unexpected activity between 'Search Products' and 'Add to Cart'.
  • Trace 4: 'Add to Cart' occurs after 'Checkout', which is out of order.
  • Trace 6: Missing 'Add to Cart' activity before 'Checkout'.
  • Trace 8: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.
  • Trace 10: 'Add to Cart' occurs before 'Search Products', which is out of order.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation—'Search Products' with resource 'Product Catalog Database' failed authorization before activation.
  • Trace 2: Resource policy violation—'Login to Account' with 'User Authentication Service' lacked required resource policy enforcement.
  • Trace 4: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 7: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 9: Binding policy violation—'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence is correct, 'Search Products' failed due to an activity policy violation involving unauthorized access to the 'Product Catalog Database'.
  • Trace 7: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Add to Cart' incorrectly used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', violating the binding policy.
  • Trace 9: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service', violating the binding policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly dependent on sequence deviations, as several traces with perfect conformance still have compliance issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, occurring in three separate traces and involving incorrect resource assignments.
  • Activity and resource policy violations are less frequent but indicate specific issues with lifecycle handling and resource management.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model change: Update the process model to enforce the correct order of 'Search Products' before 'Add to Cart' and ensure 'Add to Cart' always precedes 'Checkout'.
  • Activity policy fix: Implement strict lifecycle state checks for 'Search Products' to prevent unauthorized transitions from 'NotActivated' to 'Failed'.
  • Resource policy fix: Enforce resource policy validation for 'User Authentication Service', ensuring exclusive access and proper state transitions before reuse.
  • Binding policy fix: Restrict activities to consume only their correctly bound resources, preventing 'Add to Cart' and 'Login to Account' from accessing the 'Accounting System'.
