2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all perfect-conformance traces are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly tied to sequence deviations, as only Trace 9 has policy violations despite other traces having control-flow issues.
  • Activity and binding policy violations are both present in Trace 9, but no resource policy violations are reported.
  • Binding and activity policy violations are equally represented, with no evidence of resource policy violations in this dataset.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to explicitly handle or restrict 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce correct activity ordering, especially for 'Replace Hardware' and 'Request Support'.
  • Enforce strict lifecycle transitions for 'Request Support' so that once in 'done', it cannot return to 'activated'.
  • Review resource state management for 'Resource H' to ensure it cannot be consumed by an activity that is already completed, and implement checks to prevent resource reuse after terminal states.
  • Implement validation to ensure resources are only assigned to activities in the 'activated' state, preventing consumption by activities in any other state.
