1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: 'Approve Loan' occurs after 'Notify Applicant' (out-of-order).
  • Trace 6: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 7: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model.
  • Trace 9: Missing 'Approve Loan' or 'Reject Loan' before 'Notify Applicant'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—invalid lifecycle transition (activated→unknown).
  • Trace 2: Binding policy violations in 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource state changes occurred while activity was already done.
  • Trace 3: Resource policy violation in 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource policy was absent during use.
  • Trace 6: Binding policy violation in 'Verify Income'—resource 'Approval Board' was used instead of the bound 'Income Verification Service'.
  • Trace 7: Resource policy violations in 'Reject Loan' with 'Rejection Letter Template'—resource was overused and withdrawn without authorization.
  • Trace 8: Resource policy violation in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—resource policy was absent during use.
  • Trace 10: Activity policy violations in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—unauthorized transitions to 'compensated' and then 'done'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Verify Income' made an unauthorized lifecycle transition, violating the activity policy.
  • Trace 3: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Approve Loan' used 'Loan Approval System' without an active resource policy, violating resource policy rules.
  • Trace 8: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Verify Income' used 'Income Verification Service' without an active resource policy, violating resource policy rules.
  • Trace 10: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Verify Income' performed unauthorized lifecycle transitions to 'compensated' and then 'done'.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur both in traces with and without control-flow deviations, indicating they are independent of sequence deviations.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces and activities, especially regarding absent policies and unauthorized resource usage.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model change: Update the BPMN model to explicitly disallow 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce correct ordering between 'Approve Loan'/'Reject Loan' and 'Notify Applicant'.
  • Activity policy fix: Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for 'Verify Income' to prevent unauthorized transitions such as activated→unknown or compensated→done.
  • Resource policy fix: Ensure all resources have active, enforced policies during use, and configure usage caps and state transitions to prevent overuse or absent policy scenarios.
  • Binding policy fix: Enforce that only the correct, bound resource is assigned to an activity, and restrict resource state changes to when the activity is in the 'Activated' state.
