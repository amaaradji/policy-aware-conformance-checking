1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: 'Approve Loan' occurs after 'Notify Applicant' (out-of-order).
  • Trace 6: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model (extra activity).
  • Trace 7: Contains 'Unexpected Activity' not present in the BPMN model (extra activity).
  • Trace 9: Missing 'Approve Loan' or 'Reject Loan' activity (missing activity).

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation in 'Verify Income' using 'Income Verification Service'—unauthorized lifecycle transition.
  • Trace 2: Binding policy violations in 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource state changes occurred while activity was already done.
  • Trace 3: Resource policy violation in 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource policy was absent.
  • Trace 6: Binding policy violation in 'Verify Income'—attempted to use 'Approval Board' instead of the bound 'Income Verification Service'.
  • Trace 7: Resource policy violations in 'Reject Loan' with 'Rejection Letter Template'—resource was overused and withdrawn without authorization.
  • Trace 8: Resource policy violation in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—resource policy was absent.
  • Trace 10: Activity policy violations in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—unauthorized transitions to 'compensated' and then to 'done'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Verify Income' performed an unauthorized lifecycle transition, violating the activity policy.
  • Trace 3: The process followed the correct sequence, but 'Approve Loan' used 'Loan Approval System' without an enforced resource policy.
  • Trace 8: The activity order is correct, but 'Verify Income' used 'Income Verification Service' without a resource policy in place.
  • Trace 10: All activities are in the correct order, but 'Verify Income' made unauthorized transitions to 'compensated' and 'done' states.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur both in traces with and without control-flow deviations, indicating they are independent of sequence errors.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces regardless of activity order.
  • Binding and activity policy violations are also present but less frequent than resource policy issues.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to explicitly restrict the order of 'Approve Loan' and 'Notify Applicant', and disallow unmodeled activities such as 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for 'Verify Income', ensuring only authorized transitions (e.g., no direct activation to unknown or compensated states).
  • Enforce resource policy definitions and runtime checks for all resources, especially for 'Income Verification Service' and 'Rejection Letter Template', to prevent absent policies and unauthorized overuse or withdrawal.
  • Strengthen binding enforcement so that only the correct, pre-assigned resource can be consumed by an activity in the 'Activated' state, and prevent resource state changes after the activity is done.
