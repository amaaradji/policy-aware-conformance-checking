1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2 has conformance < 1.00 due to the missing 'Risk Assessment' activity in the sequence.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity Policy violation—'Credit Check' with resource 'Credit Bureau API' exceeded allowed retry count (more than 3 retries).
  • Trace 1: Resource Policy violation—'Credit Check' with 'Credit Bureau API' exceeded maximum allowed consumption time (over 5 minutes).
  • Trace 1: Binding Policy violation—'Credit Check' with 'Credit Bureau API' and 'Risk Assessment' with 'Risk Analyst' had invalid activity-resource state transitions (resource unlocked or done while activity was done, and consumption duration exceeded limits).

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1 has perfect conformance but is not fully compliant due to exceeding allowed retries for 'Credit Check', overusing the 'Credit Bureau API', and improper activity-resource state transitions.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of sequence deviations, as seen in Trace 1, which follows the correct activity order but still violates multiple policies.
  • Activity Policy violations are the most common, particularly related to exceeding retry limits for retriable activities.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to enforce mandatory execution of 'Risk Assessment' after 'Credit Check' to prevent missing activities.
  • Implement strict enforcement of retry limits for retriable activities like 'Credit Check', rejecting further activation attempts after the maximum count is reached.
  • Configure the 'Credit Bureau API' resource to automatically lock or reject requests that exceed the allowed usage time or retry cap, and ensure it transitions to 'Withdrawn' after reaching 'Done'.
  • Enforce binding rules so that resources can only be consumed by activities in the 'Activated' state, and prevent unlocking or marking resources as 'Done' while the associated activity is already 'Done'.
