3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly tied to sequence deviations, as Trace 9 has both a sequence deviation and policy violations, but other traces with sequence issues do not show policy violations.
  • Binding policy and activity policy violations are observed, both occurring in Trace 9, with no resource policy violations present in this dataset.
  • Binding policy violations are as frequent as activity policy violations in the observed violations, each appearing once in Trace 9.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to explicitly handle or restrict 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce correct activity order, especially ensuring 'Request Support' precedes all other activities.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for Pivot activities to prevent unauthorized transitions such as 'done' to 'activated'.
  • Review resource management logic to ensure resources are not consumed by activities that have already reached a terminal state.
  • Enforce binding rules so that only activities in the 'activated' state can consume resources, rejecting any binding attempts from activities in 'done' or other invalid states.
