  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all perfectly conforming traces are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly dependent on sequence deviations, as traces with both correct and incorrect activity orders exhibit resource policy violations.
  • Resource policy violations are the only type observed in this dataset, making them the most common violation type.
  • No activity or binding policy violations were reported, indicating resource handling is the primary compliance issue.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to explicitly prohibit 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce correct activity order, ensuring all required activities are present.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle state checks for all activities to guarantee transitions follow the defined state machine.
  • Enforce exclusive access and correct state transitions for NonShareableUnlimited resources like 'Worker E', and ensure usage caps are respected for limited resources such as 'Resource H'.
  • Restrict resource consumption to only those activities in the 'Activated' state to comply with binding policy rules.
