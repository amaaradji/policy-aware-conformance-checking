  • Trace 5: Although the activity sequence matches the model, the activity 'Notify Team' violated its lifecycle policy by attempting a failed->activated transition not allowed for Pivot activities.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations; traces can have perfect conformance but still violate resource or binding policies.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, occurring in Trace 6 and Trace 10 for the same activity-resource pair.
  • Control-flow deviations and policy violations do not necessarily overlap, as seen in Trace 2 (sequence issue) versus Traces 5, 6, and 10 (policy issues).

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to explicitly exclude or handle 'Unexpected Activity' to prevent sequence deviations.
  • Revise the lifecycle management for 'Notify Team' to prevent unauthorized failed->activated transitions for Pivot activities.
  • Enforce resource access controls for 'System A' and 'Database C', ensuring transitions and usage adhere to their policy constraints and state requirements.
  • Implement mandatory binding policies for all activity-resource assignments, especially for 'Assign Priority' with 'Database C', to ensure proper consumption and state tracking.
