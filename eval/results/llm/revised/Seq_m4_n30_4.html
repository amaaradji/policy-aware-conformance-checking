1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Missing activity 'Add to Cart'.
  • Trace 8: Missing activity 'Search Products'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 2: [Binding Policy] - Activity 'Add to Cart' used resource 'Finance Clerk' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 5: [Binding Policy] - Activity 'Add to Cart' completed while resource 'Shopping Cart Service' was unlocked, violating binding state rules.
  • Trace 7: [Activity Policy] - Activity 'Add to Cart' lacked an activity policy for 'Shopping Cart Service', violating lifecycle authorization.
  • Trace 8: [Activity Policy] - Activity 'Checkout' with resource 'Payment Gateway' took unauthorized transitions: notactivated->unknown and unknown->activated.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 2: The main violation was using a resource ('Finance Clerk') not bound to the activity 'Add to Cart', breaching binding policy rules.
  • Trace 5: The activity 'Add to Cart' completed while its resource ('Shopping Cart Service') was unlocked, violating the binding policy for proper state management.
  • Trace 7: The activity 'Add to Cart' lacked an activity policy for its resource, resulting in unauthorized lifecycle transitions despite correct sequence.
  • Trace 8: The activity 'Checkout' with 'Payment Gateway' took unauthorized lifecycle transitions, causing compliance issues even though the sequence was valid.
  • Trace 5 and Trace 7 had binding and activity policy violations, respectively, without sequence deviations.
  • Trace 2 had a binding policy violation, but no control-flow deviation.
  • Trace 8 had activity policy violations, but also a sequence deviation.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur independently of control-flow deviations, as several traces with perfect sequence still have compliance issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces where resource assignment or state transitions were incorrect.
  • Activity policy violations also appear, particularly in traces where lifecycle transitions were unauthorized, but resource policy violations are absent in this dataset.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce mandatory inclusion of 'Add to Cart' and 'Search Products' in all traces to prevent missing activities.
  • Implement explicit lifecycle state handlers for Compensatable activities, ensuring only authorized transitions (e.g., notactivated->activated->done/failed) are possible.
  • Configure resource 'Shopping Cart Service' to prevent unlocking or state changes while the consuming activity is not in the 'Activated' state, enforcing proper access patterns.
  • Enforce strict binding rules so activities can only use their assigned resources, and ensure resource state changes are synchronized with activity completion.
