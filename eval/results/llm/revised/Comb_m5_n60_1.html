2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: The 'Verify Income' activity with 'Income Verification Service' performed an unauthorized lifecycle transition, violating its activity policy.
  • Trace 3: The 'Approve Loan' activity with 'Loan Approval System' lacked an enforced resource policy, breaching resource policy requirements.
  • Trace 8: The 'Verify Income' activity with 'Income Verification Service' lacked an enforced resource policy, breaching resource policy requirements.
  • Trace 10: The 'Verify Income' activity with 'Income Verification Service' performed unauthorized lifecycle transitions, violating its activity policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur both with and without control-flow deviations, indicating they are independent of sequence deviations.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces and activities, especially regarding absent policies and unauthorized state transitions.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the process model to explicitly handle and restrict the order of 'Approve Loan' and ensure 'Reject Loan' is included where required, and remove or properly integrate 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Review and correct lifecycle transition handlers for 'Verify Income' to only allow authorized transitions (e.g., prevent 'activated->unknown' and 'activated->compensated').
  • Resource Policy Fix: Define and enforce resource policies for all resources, especially 'Loan Approval System' and 'Income Verification Service', to prevent absent policies and unauthorized state transitions.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Ensure activities always use their bound resources and prevent resource state changes (lock, consume, unlock) while the activity is not in the correct state (e.g., not 'done').
