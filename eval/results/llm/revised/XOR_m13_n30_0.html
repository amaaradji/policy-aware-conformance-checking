1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Missing 'Close Ticket'.
  • Trace 3: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 4: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 6: 'Request Support' is out of order.
  • Trace 9: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 10: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 9: [BINDING POLICY] - Activity 'Request Support' used resource 'Resource H' (ShareableLimitedBR) after the activity was done, violating the binding policy by changing resource state while not activated.
  • Trace 9: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - Activity 'Request Support' with resource 'Resource H' (ShareableLimitedBR) performed a done→activated transition, which is not authorized for Pivot activities.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have perfect conformance (1.00) with compliance less than 1.00; all traces with conformance = 1.00 also have compliance = 1.00.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not always tied to control-flow deviations; for example, Trace 9 has both, but other traces with control-flow issues do not show policy violations.
  • Binding policy and activity policy violations are observed in Trace 9, with binding policy violations being the most frequent among the detected policy issues.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the process model to explicitly handle and constrain 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce the correct order for 'Request Support' and 'Close Ticket'.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Restrict 'Request Support' (Pivot) so that once it reaches 'done', it cannot transition back to 'activated' under any circumstance.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Ensure that 'Resource H' (ShareableLimitedBR) cannot be consumed or have its state changed while the associated activity is not in the 'Activated' state.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Enforce that resource state transitions for 'Request Support' only occur during the activity's 'Activated' phase, and prevent resource usage after the activity is marked as 'done'.
