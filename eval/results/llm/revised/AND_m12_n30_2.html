3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations, as some traces have perfect conformance but still show compliance issues.
  • Binding Policy violations are the most common, occurring in both Trace 6 and Trace 10.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to explicitly handle or restrict the occurrence of 'Unexpected Activity' to prevent unmodeled steps.
  • Review and enforce correct lifecycle transitions for Pivot activities, ensuring 'failed' to 'activated' transitions are blocked unless explicitly allowed.
  • Audit resource access patterns for 'System A' and 'Database C' to ensure all state transitions and usage limits comply with their respective resource policies.
  • Implement and enforce binding policies for all activity-resource pairs, especially for 'Assign Priority' with 'Database C', to ensure valid assignments and state management during execution.
