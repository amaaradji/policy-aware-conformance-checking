1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Missing 'Close Ticket'.
  • Trace 3: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 4: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 6: 'Request Support' occurred out of order.
  • Trace 9: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 10: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 9: [BINDING POLICY] violation—activity 'Request Support' used resource 'Resource H' (ShareableLimitedBR) after the activity was done, causing a resource state change while not activated.
  • Trace 9: [ACTIVITY POLICY] violation—activity 'Request Support' with resource 'Resource H' (ShareableLimitedBR) performed a done→activated transition, which is not authorized for Pivot activities.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all traces with perfect conformance are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are not strictly tied to sequence deviations, as Trace 9 has both control-flow and policy violations, but other traces with sequence issues do not show policy violations.
  • Binding policy and activity policy violations are observed, both occurring in Trace 9; no resource policy violations are present.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common among the observed policy violations in this dataset.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model change: Update the process model to explicitly allow or handle 'Unexpected Activity' and enforce the correct order for 'Request Support' and 'Close Ticket'.
  • Activity policy fix: Enforce that Pivot activities like 'Request Support' cannot transition from 'done' back to 'activated' by validating lifecycle transitions in the workflow engine.
  • Resource policy fix: Review and restrict resource state transitions to prevent consumption changes when the activity is not in the 'Activated' state, especially for shareable resources.
  • Binding policy fix: Ensure that resource consumption for an activity only occurs during its 'Activated' state and that resources are released or locked according to the binding constraints before activity completion.
