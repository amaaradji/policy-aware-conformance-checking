1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: 'Approve Loan' occurred out of order.
  • Trace 6: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' was executed.
  • Trace 7: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' was executed.
  • Trace 9: 'Reject Loan' was missing.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—an unauthorized lifecycle transition was attempted.
  • Trace 2: Binding policy violations in 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—resource state transitions occurred while the activity was already done.
  • Trace 3: Resource policy violation in 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System'—the required resource policy was absent.
  • Trace 6: Binding policy violation in 'Verify Income'—resource 'Approval Board' was used instead of the bound 'Income Verification Service'.
  • Trace 7: Resource policy violations in 'Reject Loan' with 'Rejection Letter Template'—unauthorized overuse and withdrawal transitions occurred.
  • Trace 8: Resource policy violation in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—the required resource policy was absent.
  • Trace 10: Activity policy violations in 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service'—unauthorized transitions to and from the 'compensated' state were performed.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matched the model, 'Verify Income' performed an unauthorized lifecycle transition, violating its activity policy.
  • Trace 3: The process followed the correct order, but 'Approve Loan' was executed without a required resource policy, violating compliance.
  • Trace 8: The activity sequence was correct, but 'Verify Income' used 'Income Verification Service' without an associated resource policy.
  • Trace 10: All activities were in order, but 'Verify Income' performed unauthorized transitions involving the 'compensated' state.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations and sequence deviations are independent, as several traces have policy violations despite perfect control-flow conformance.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces across different activities and resources.
  • Binding and activity policy violations also occur but are less frequent than resource policy issues.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model change: Update the process model to enforce correct ordering of 'Approve Loan' and ensure 'Reject Loan' is included where required.
  • Activity policy fix: Review and enforce lifecycle transition rules for 'Verify Income', especially restricting unauthorized transitions to or from 'compensated' and unknown states.
  • Resource policy fix: Define and implement explicit resource policies for all resources, including 'Loan Approval System' and 'Income Verification Service', and prevent unauthorized state transitions such as overuse or withdrawal.
  • Binding policy fix: Ensure activities only use their bound resources and prevent resource state changes (lock, consume, unlock) after the activity is marked as done.
