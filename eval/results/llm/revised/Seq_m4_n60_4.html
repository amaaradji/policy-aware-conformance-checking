3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur both in traces with and without control-flow deviations, indicating they are independent of sequence issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, appearing in three traces and involving incorrect resource assignment.
  • Activity and resource policy violations are less frequent and typically relate to lifecycle or resource configuration errors.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce correct activity order, ensuring 'Add to Cart' always precedes 'Checkout' and 'Search Products'.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle transition checks for 'Search Products', preventing unauthorized notactivated->failed transitions in Retriable activities.
  • Define and enforce resource policies for 'User Authentication Service', ensuring all access patterns and state transitions are properly governed.
  • Revise activity-resource assignment rules to prevent activities from using resources other than their bound ones, especially disallowing 'Accounting System' for 'Add to Cart' and 'Login to Account'.
