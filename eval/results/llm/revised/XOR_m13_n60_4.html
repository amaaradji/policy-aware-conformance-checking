  • Trace 5: Contains unexpected 'Unexpected Activity'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: [RESOURCE POLICY] violation—activity 'Close Ticket', resource 'Worker E'; resource policy is absent.
  • Trace 3: [RESOURCE POLICY] violation—activity 'Request Support', resource 'Resource H'; resource policy is absent.
  • Trace 9: [RESOURCE POLICY] violation—activity 'Close Ticket', resource 'Worker E'; resource policy is absent.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all perfectly conformant traces are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur independently of control-flow sequence deviations, as some traces with sequence issues do not have policy violations and vice versa.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, specifically due to absent resource policies for certain activities and resources.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the process model to explicitly allow or restrict 'Unexpected Activity' and ensure 'Request Support' and 'Close Ticket' are mandatory where required.
  • Review and enforce lifecycle transitions for all activities to ensure they follow the authorized sequence (e.g., NotActivated → Activated → Done/Failed).
  • Define and implement resource policies for all resources, especially for 'Worker E' and 'Resource H', to control access patterns and state transitions during activity execution.
  • Establish clear binding rules to ensure activities only use their assigned resources and that resource state changes are tracked and enforced during activity consumption.
