1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: 'Approve Loan' occurred out of order.
  • Trace 6: 'Unexpected Activity' was executed unexpectedly.
  • Trace 7: 'Unexpected Activity' was executed unexpectedly.
  • Trace 9: 'Reject Loan' was missing.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service' had an unauthorized lifecycle transition (activated->unknown).
  • Trace 2: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System' had multiple unauthorized resource state transitions while activity was done.
  • Trace 3: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System' lacked a resource policy during consumption.
  • Trace 6: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Verify Income' used 'Approval Board' instead of the bound 'Income Verification Service'.
  • Trace 7: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Reject Loan' with 'Rejection Letter Template' had unauthorized transitions: notconsumed->overused and overused->withdrawn.
  • Trace 8: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service' lacked a resource policy during consumption.
  • Trace 10: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service' had unauthorized transitions: activated->compensated and compensated->done.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: The activity 'Verify Income' violated its activity policy by taking an unauthorized lifecycle transition despite following the correct sequence.
  • Trace 3: The activity 'Approve Loan' violated its resource policy due to the absence of a defined policy for the 'Loan Approval System'.
  • Trace 8: The activity 'Verify Income' violated its resource policy because 'Income Verification Service' lacked a resource policy during consumption.
  • Trace 10: The activity 'Verify Income' violated its activity policy by taking unauthorized transitions (activated->compensated and compensated->done) even though the sequence was correct.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations, as several traces with perfect conformance still exhibit policy violations.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, appearing in traces 3, 7, and 8.
  • Binding and activity policy violations also occur, but less frequently than resource policy violations.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce correct ordering of 'Approve Loan' and ensure 'Reject Loan' is included where required; remove or handle 'Unexpected Activity' explicitly.
  • Implement stricter lifecycle transition checks for 'Verify Income', ensuring only authorized transitions (e.g., activated->done or activated->failed) are allowed for Retriable activities.
  • Define and enforce resource policies for all resources, especially 'Loan Approval System' and 'Income Verification Service', to prevent unauthorized consumption and state transitions.
  • Restrict activity-resource bindings so that activities can only use their assigned resources, and ensure resource state changes occur only when the activity is properly activated or completed.
