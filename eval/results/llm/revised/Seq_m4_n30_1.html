1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Missing activity 'Add to Cart'.
  • Trace 8: Missing activity 'Search Products'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 2: Binding policy violation in 'Add to Cart' with 'Shopping Cart Service'—activity used resource 'Finance Clerk' instead of the bound resource.
  • Trace 5: Binding policy violation in 'Add to Cart' with 'Shopping Cart Service'—resource was unlocked and transitioned to 'done' while activity was still 'done'.
  • Trace 7: Activity policy violation in 'Add to Cart' with 'Shopping Cart Service'—activity policy was absent.
  • Trace 8: Activity policy violations in 'Checkout' with 'Payment Gateway'—unauthorized transitions from 'notactivated' to 'unknown' and from 'unknown' to 'activated'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 2: Despite following the correct activity sequence, 'Add to Cart' used an incorrect resource, violating the binding policy.
  • Trace 5: The activity sequence was correct, but 'Add to Cart' violated the binding policy by improper resource state transitions during completion.
  • Trace 7: The activity sequence was correct, but 'Add to Cart' lacked an activity policy, violating lifecycle authorization.
  • Trace 8: The activity sequence was correct, but 'Checkout' had unauthorized lifecycle transitions, violating the activity policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations, as several traces with perfect activity sequences still exhibit policy violations.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces where resource assignment or state transitions were incorrect.
  • Activity policy violations also occur but are less frequent than binding policy violations.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce mandatory inclusion of 'Add to Cart' and 'Search Products' in every trace to prevent missing activities.
  • Implement explicit lifecycle state checks for Compensatable activities, ensuring only authorized transitions (e.g., 'notactivated' to 'activated', 'activated' to 'done').
  • Configure 'Shopping Cart Service' to restrict unlocking and state transitions to 'done' only when the consuming activity has fully completed and released the resource.
  • Enforce strict activity-resource binding rules so that 'Add to Cart' can only use the assigned 'Shopping Cart Service' and not substitute with other resources like 'Finance Clerk'.
