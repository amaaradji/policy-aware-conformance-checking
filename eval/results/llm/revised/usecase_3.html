  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations, as Trace 1 follows the correct activity sequence but still exhibits several policy breaches.

  • Resource and binding policy violations also occur but are less frequent compared to activity policy breaches.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce mandatory execution of 'Risk Assessment' after 'Credit Check' to prevent missing activities.
  • Implement stricter retry logic in 'Credit Check' to ensure the activity cannot be reactivated more than three times.
  • Configure 'Credit Bureau API' to automatically lock and release resources within the allowed five-minute window, rejecting overlong consumption attempts.
  • Revise binding rules so that resources cannot change state (e.g., unlock or done) while the associated activity is completed, ensuring proper synchronization between activity and resource states.
