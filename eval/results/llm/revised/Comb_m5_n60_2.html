1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: 'Approve Loan' occurred out of order.
  • Trace 6: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' was executed.
  • Trace 7: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' was executed.
  • Trace 9: 'Reject Loan' was missing.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service' performed an unauthorized lifecycle transition (activated->unknown).
  • Trace 2: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System' had resource state changes (locked->consumed, consumed->unlocked, unlocked->done) while the activity was already done.
  • Trace 3: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Approve Loan' with 'Loan Approval System' lacked a resource policy definition during execution.
  • Trace 6: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Verify Income' used 'Approval Board' instead of the bound 'Income Verification Service'.
  • Trace 7: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Reject Loan' with 'Rejection Letter Template' performed unauthorized transitions (notconsumed->overused and overused->withdrawn).
  • Trace 8: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service' lacked a resource policy definition during execution.
  • Trace 10: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - 'Verify Income' with 'Income Verification Service' performed unauthorized lifecycle transitions (activated->compensated and compensated->done).

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: The activity 'Verify Income' performed an unauthorized lifecycle transition (activated->unknown) with 'Income Verification Service', violating its activity policy.
  • Trace 3: The activity 'Approve Loan' used 'Loan Approval System' without a defined resource policy, violating resource policy requirements.
  • Trace 8: The activity 'Verify Income' used 'Income Verification Service' without a defined resource policy, violating resource policy requirements.
  • Trace 10: The activity 'Verify Income' performed unauthorized lifecycle transitions (activated->compensated and compensated->done), violating its activity policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations and sequence deviations are largely independent, as several traces with perfect conformance still exhibit policy violations.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, affecting multiple traces due to absent policies or unauthorized resource state transitions.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the process model to explicitly restrict the order of 'Approve Loan' and ensure 'Reject Loan' is included where required, and remove or handle 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Review and enforce correct lifecycle transitions for 'Verify Income', ensuring only authorized transitions (e.g., activated->done or activated->failed) are possible for Retriable activities.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Define and implement resource policies for all resources, especially 'Loan Approval System' and 'Income Verification Service', to prevent unauthorized or undefined state transitions and usage patterns.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Enforce strict resource binding so that activities only use their assigned resources, and prevent resource state changes (lock, consume, unlock) after the activity is marked as done.
