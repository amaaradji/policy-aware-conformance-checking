1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains unexpected activity 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 4: 'Checkout' occurs out of order before 'Add to Cart'.
  • Trace 6: Missing activity 'Add to Cart'.
  • Trace 8: 'Add to Cart' occurs out of order before 'Search Products'.
  • Trace 10: 'Add to Cart' occurs out of order before 'Search Products'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: [ACTIVITY POLICY] - 'Search Products' with resource 'Product Catalog Database' performed a notactivated->failed transition, which is not authorized for Retriable activities.
  • Trace 2: [RESOURCE POLICY] - 'Login to Account' with resource 'User Authentication Service' lacked a resource policy definition for a NonShareableLimited resource.
  • Trace 4: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Add to Cart' used resource 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 7: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Add to Cart' used resource 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service'.
  • Trace 9: [BINDING POLICY] - 'Login to Account' used resource 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service'.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence is correct, 'Search Products' performed an unauthorized notactivated->failed transition, violating the activity policy for Retriable activities.
  • Trace 9: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service', violating the binding policy.
  • Trace 7: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', violating the binding policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations occur both with and without control-flow deviations, indicating they are independent issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, appearing in Traces 4, 7, and 9.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the process model to enforce the correct order of 'Add to Cart' before 'Checkout' and prevent unexpected activities.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Ensure that Retriable activities like 'Search Products' cannot transition directly from notactivated to failed without first being activated.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Define and enforce resource policies for all NonShareableLimited resources, especially for 'User Authentication Service', to prevent unauthorized or undefined usage.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Restrict activities to use only their bound resources by validating resource assignments before execution, especially for 'Add to Cart' and 'Login to Account'.
