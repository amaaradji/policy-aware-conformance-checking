1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 2: Contains unexpected activity 'Unexpected Activity'.
  • Trace 4: Activity 'Checkout' occurs out of order.
  • Trace 6: Missing activity 'Add to Cart'.
  • Trace 8: Activity 'Add to Cart' occurs out of order.
  • Trace 10: Activity 'Add to Cart' occurs out of order.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: Activity policy violation—'Search Products' (Product Catalog Database) performed a notactivated→failed transition, which is not authorized for Retriable activities.
  • Trace 2: Resource policy violation—'Login to Account' (User Authentication Service) lacked a resource policy during execution.
  • Trace 4: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' (Shopping Cart Service) used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound resource.
  • Trace 7: Binding policy violation—'Add to Cart' (Shopping Cart Service) used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound resource.
  • Trace 9: Binding policy violation—'Login to Account' (User Authentication Service) used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound resource.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • Trace 1: Although the activity sequence matches the model, 'Search Products' performed a notactivated→failed transition, violating its activity policy.
  • Trace 7: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Add to Cart' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'Shopping Cart Service', breaching the binding policy.
  • Trace 9: The activity sequence is correct, but 'Login to Account' used 'Accounting System' instead of the bound 'User Authentication Service', violating the binding policy.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of control-flow deviations, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but binding or activity policy breaches.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, appearing in traces 4, 7, and 9.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model change: Update the process model to allow 'Add to Cart' and 'Checkout' to occur in flexible order where business rules permit, and explicitly disallow unexpected activities.
  • Activity policy fix: Ensure 'Search Products' (Retriable) can only transition to 'failed' after being properly activated, and enforce correct lifecycle transitions in the implementation.
  • Resource policy fix: Define and enforce a resource policy for 'User Authentication Service' to control access and state transitions during 'Login to Account'.
  • Binding policy fix: Restrict activities to use only their bound resources by validating resource assignments before execution, preventing use of 'Accounting System' where 'Shopping Cart Service' or 'User Authentication Service' is required.
