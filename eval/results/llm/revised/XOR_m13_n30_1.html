1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Missing 'Close Ticket' activity.
  • Trace 3: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' occurred.
  • Trace 4: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' occurred.
  • Trace 6: 'Request Support' activity executed out of order.
  • Trace 9: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' occurred.
  • Trace 10: Unexpected 'Unexpected Activity' occurred.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 9: [BINDING POLICY] violation in 'Request Support' with resource 'Resource H'—resource was consumed while the activity was already done.
  • Trace 9: [ACTIVITY POLICY] violation in 'Request Support' with resource 'Resource H'—activity transitioned from done to activated, which is not authorized.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have perfect conformance (1.00) and non-perfect compliance (<1.00); all traces with conformance = 1.00 also have compliance = 1.00.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations are independent of sequence deviations, as Trace 9 has both control-flow and policy violations while other traces with sequence issues do not.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common among the policy violations observed in this dataset.
  • Activity policy violations also occur, but only in Trace 9, and no resource policy violations are present.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Update the BPMN model to enforce the presence and correct ordering of 'Close Ticket' and restrict 'Unexpected Activity' to valid paths only.
  • Review and adjust lifecycle handling for 'Request Support' to prevent unauthorized transitions from 'done' to 'activated'.
  • Implement stricter resource access controls for 'Resource H' to ensure it cannot be consumed when the associated activity is not in the 'Activated' state.
  • Revise binding rules so that resource consumption is only permitted while the activity is active, and ensure resource assignment does not extend beyond activity completion.
