1. PROBLEMATIC TRACES - TWO CATEGORIES

A. Control-Flow Issues

  • Trace 1: Unexpected activity 'Unexpected Activity' occurred.
  • Trace 3: 'Request Support' was performed out of order.
  • Trace 4: Missing activity 'Request Support'.
  • Trace 5: Unexpected activity 'Unexpected Activity' occurred.
  • Trace 8: Missing activity 'Close Ticket'.
  • Trace 9: 'Request Support' was performed out of order.
  • Trace 10: Missing activity 'Close Ticket'.

B. Policy Violations

  • Trace 1: [RESOURCE POLICY] - Activity 'Close Ticket' used resource 'Worker E' without a defined resource policy.
  • Trace 3: [RESOURCE POLICY] - Activity 'Request Support' used resource 'Resource H' without a defined resource policy.
  • Trace 9: [RESOURCE POLICY] - Activity 'Close Ticket' used resource 'Worker E' without a defined resource policy.

2. PERFECT-CONFORMANCE BUT NON-COMPLIANT TRACES

  • No traces have conformance = 1.00 and compliance < 1.00; all perfectly conformant traces are also fully compliant.

3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations and control-flow deviations occur independently, as some traces have only one type of issue.
  • Resource policy violations are the most common, specifically the absence of a resource policy for certain activities and resources.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the process model to explicitly allow or handle 'Unexpected Activity' and ensure 'Request Support' and 'Close Ticket' are always included where required.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Review and enforce correct lifecycle transitions for all activities, ensuring each activity follows its authorized state sequence.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Define and implement resource policies for all resources, especially for 'Worker E' and 'Resource H', to control their usage and state transitions during activity execution.
  • Binding Policy Fix: Ensure that every activity-resource assignment is governed by a binding policy, preventing resource use without proper policy definition and enforcement.
