3. CORRELATION ANALYSIS

  • Policy violations can occur independently of control-flow deviations, as seen in traces with perfect conformance but binding or activity policy issues.
  • Binding policy violations are the most common, affecting traces 4, 7, and 9.
  • Control-flow and policy violations do not always overlap, indicating separate root causes.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS

  • Model Change: Update the BPMN model to allow 'Add to Cart' and 'Checkout' to occur in either order if business rules permit, or enforce strict sequencing if not.
  • Activity Policy Fix: Review and correct lifecycle handling for 'Search Products' to ensure only authorized transitions (e.g., notactivated→activated or failed) are possible, and retriable logic is properly implemented.
  • Resource Policy Fix: Define and enforce a resource policy for 'User Authentication Service' to ensure all access patterns and state transitions are explicitly governed.
